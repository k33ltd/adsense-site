Chase Cards: Eligibility

How 5/24 Works

  • Rule: Chase denies most applications if you've opened 5+ personal credit cards (any bank) in the last 24 months.
  • Counts: Personal cards from Chase, Amex, Capital One, Citi, BofA, Discover, store cards, credit union cards.
  • Usually doesn't count: Most business cards (Chase Ink, Amex Business, Capital One Spark, Citi Business), authorized user accounts, charge cards without preset limit (Amex Platinum/Gold *personal* do count).
  • Timing: Based on *approval date*, not application date. 24-month rolling window.
  • Strategy: If you're at 4/24, apply for Chase cards *first* before any non-Chase cards.

Recommended Chase Cards by Profile

Once you're under 5/24, here's the typical application order:
