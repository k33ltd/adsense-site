How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select your points currency (tab at top).
  2. Enter your current balance and which card you hold (affects cash value).
  3. Review the valuation cards — the green "Best" card shows your highest-value option.
  4. Scan the transfer table for ⭐ sweet spots matching your travel goals.

Valuation Methodology

  • Cash value: Based on card's portal rate (Chase) or statement credit rate (Amex/CapOne/Bilt).
  • Transfer value: Based on Head for Points 2024 valuations + award availability data. Ranges reflect saver vs. peak.
  • Sweet spots flagged: Partners where transfer value consistently exceeds cash value by 30%+.
  • Updated: January 2024. Valuations change; verify before transferring.
