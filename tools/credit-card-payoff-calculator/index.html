How It Works

This calculator uses the standard amortization formula for revolving credit:

n = -log(1 - (r × B) / P) / log(1 + r)
Where: r = monthly rate (APR/12), B = balance, P = monthly payment

Assumptions:

  • Interest compounds monthly (most common for credit cards)
  • No new charges added during payoff
  • Payment applies to interest first, then principal
  • Minimum payment = max(interest + 1% of balance, $25) — but you enter your actual planned payment

Tips to Pay Off Faster

  • Pay more than minimum: Even $50 extra/month saves years and thousands in interest
  • Target highest APR first (avalanche): Mathematically optimal
  • Consider 0% balance transfer: Pause interest for 12-21 months (watch 3-5% fee)
  • Automate payments: Never miss a due date — late fees + penalty APR kill progress
