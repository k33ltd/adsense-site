How We Make Money

[SITE NAME] is a free resource supported by two revenue streams:

1. Affiliate Commissions

We partner with credit card networks and comparison platforms (including CardRatings, CreditCards.com, Bankrate, and direct issuer programs). When you click an application link and are approved, we may earn a commission.

  • Commissions range from $50–$300+ per approved application, depending on the card and network.

2. Display Advertising (Google AdSense)

We display ads via Google AdSense. These ads are personalized based on your browsing history and interests. We do not control which specific ads appear.

  • Ad revenue is based on impressions and clicks.
  • Advertisers do not influence our editorial content.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.

What This Means for You

  • No extra cost: Commissions are paid by the issuer/network, not by you. The offer terms are identical whether you click our link or go directly.
  • Same offers: We link to the best publicly available offer. Sometimes we negotiate a better bonus for our readers.
  • No pay-to-play: We have never and will never accept payment for placement, positive reviews, or ranking manipulation.

Our Review Methodology

Every "Best X" guide follows this process:

  1. Universe definition: Identify all relevant products in the category (e.g., all personal travel cards with annual fee < $550).
  2. Data collection: Pull 40+ data points per product from issuer terms, public benefit guides, and award charts.
  3. Modeling: Calculate net annual value for 3 spend profiles (Light/Moderate/Heavy) across categories.
  4. Qualitative assessment: Factor in transfer partner quality, award availability, customer service reputation, app UX.
  5. Real-world testing: Team members hold or have held most recommended cards. Redemptions documented.
  6. Ranking: Sort by net value for each profile. Top pick = highest net value for the majority profile.
  7. Disclosure check: Verify no conflict (e.g., we don't own issuer stock, no personal relationships).

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ Accept free products in exchange for reviews
  • ❌ Let advertisers review content before publication
  • ❌ Hide negative aspects to preserve a partnership
  • ❌ Recommend cards we wouldn't use ourselves (or haven't thoroughly vetted)
  • ❌ Use "best" loosely — every superlative is backed by the math above
